Dr. Elena Vance

Assistant Professor of Computational Neuroscience & AI
Department of Brain & Cognitive Sciences, Meridian Institute of Technology

I lead the Neural Geometry & Inference Lab. My research investigates how biological and artificial neural networks learn structured representations, generalize from sparse experience, and perform robust causal inference under uncertainty.

Before joining Meridian, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Gatsby Computational Neuroscience Unit and completed my PhD in Computer Science at ETH Zürich, advised by Prof. Henrik Lindqvist.

Research Agenda

01 / CORE THEMES
THEME 01

Representational Geometry

Uncovering how cortical manifolds curve and disentangle sensory variables to enable rapid compositional generalization.

THEME 02

Energy-Efficient Learning

Designing local, biologically plausible credit-assignment rules that rival backpropagation without weight transport.

THEME 03

Interpretability in Foundation Models

Applying tools from systems neuroscience to probe latent world models inside large-scale generative transformers.
